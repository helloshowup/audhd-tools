// HOW TO READ IT

Your tasks come from the Five Days store, and your events come from Google Calendar. Both are read for today each time this page opens. The other days show this device's last read until you fetch them. Ticking the box left of a task name marks it done in the Five Days store.

The strips at the top

  • STATUSThe notice strip. It appears only when something needs you, and it says so in plain words. Two examples: when part of the read failed, it names the part and says what still works; when a press did not save, it says NOT SAVED. The notice, the Saving line and the Undo bar stack at the top and stay in view while you scroll, and none covers another.
  • CATCHThe catch box. In the top bar, on every tab. Type a thought and press Catch (or Enter); key c puts the cursor in it on a keyboard. It is saved at once as a task dated today and sits in Caught at the top of Today, left out of the counts and the forecast. The next tidy pass (05:45, 11:45 or 17:45) gives it a topic and its note lines, and then it joins your other cards. If the store does not save it, the words stay in the box and the notice strip says NOT SAVED.
  • WEATHERWeather. The day's low and high in Cape Town, then a picture and a temperature for each block of hours. It comes from your Cape Town weather calendar (forecast by Meteomatics). A dashed box means there is no ND picture yet for that weather: tell Claude.
  • SOCIALND Agency social. This band appears only on a day with a post: one due today, one not posted on its day, or one you posted today. For each platform, Words and picture copies the words and hands the picture to the phone's share sheet. You post it yourself in that app, then press the Orange Posted button, which writes one line on the post's task. Under "Words and other ways" you can copy the words alone or save the picture. The page never posts for you.

Task cards

  • Solid, with a shadow. Ready to do.
  • PAYThe tag in a card's corner. What kind of card it is: WhatsApp, Email, Call, LinkedIn, Job, Digest, Pick one, Pay, Errand, Form or Build. Under the title the card shows the one thing that kind is about: your reply under their message, the To and Thread of an email, the number to ring, the person and the verb, the job's ledger, how many finds, the options with Pick this, the amount, the place and what to get, the days until a form closes, what is being built and what done looks like. The kind is the note's "Card:" line; when a task has none, the page guesses, and the tidy pass fills it in. A card with no tag is a plain card.
  • PAIDDone today. A folded band at the foot of Today. A ticked card leaves its place at once and waits there, folded to one mark that says what happened, read from the note's first line: green when it was done, slate when it closed another way. Undo (ten seconds) reopens a tick.
  • WHO?who? The tidy pass found a name or term in the task it could not match to one person in Claude's memory. The line "Who is <name>?" below the note's eight fixed lines says who it could be, or that nobody is known.
  • WAITING 3 DAYSwaiting N days. How long the card has been blocked (⛔ or a filled "Blocked by:" line), counted from the day the page first saw it blocked. Kept in the page's shared store, so every device counts the same; it clears once the blocker is gone.
  • MOVED 3 TIMESmoved N times. How often +1 day was pressed on this task, shown from the third press. A count only; the page never says why.
  • Deep AILight, Standard or Deep AI. How strong an AI the task needs, judged each hour by the prompt writer from the task's title and note. One filled square is Light, two Standard, three Deep. Close out also says how much thinking to ask for: Low, Medium, High or Max effort. The card names no model: which one you use for each strength is your choice. No mark means the prompt writer has not judged the task yet.
  • Dashed, no shadow. Blocked by another task or event. It turns solid once that task is ticked or that event has passed.
  • HANDSOn a day with no plan yet, your cards are banded by what your hands do to finish them (Say yes, Send a message, Write a thing, Pick one, Pay or move money, Sit at a screen, Get up and do it, Unsorted, then Idea bank, left out of the counts and the forecast). Each band's colour marks its heading and its forecast squares on the strip; Unsorted has no fill. Tap any band's heading, here or With an AI, Still open from earlier and Parked, to fold it to one row or open it; Idea bank starts folded. The fold is saved for every device and every day tab.
  • PROJECTA project's tag. A task in one of your projects wears the project's short tag (ROOM FIT-OUT, DGMT) in place of the area tag (ND AGENCY). The tag's colour is its heading's, such as The room or Funding. Tap the tag to see all of that project's open steps on the Projects tab.
  • PROJECTSThe Projects tab. The sixth tab (key 6). Each project you kept shows what finished means, the date it is needed by, and three steps in order: Now (the first step nothing blocks), Next and Then, each with its day and whose move it is. Steps opens the whole list. Orange Start a project at the top opens a short form: the name, what finished means (one sentence someone else could check), the first step if you know it, its heading and the date it is needed by. Orange Add a step on a project types one step in; it is saved as a task dated today with the project on its Group line. A project whose open steps all wait on someone else, an AI or a date says Waiting on who or what. A project with no step you can do now and nothing to wait on says No next step, offers Add a step and the Green Next step prompt for an AI chat, and is counted on the line under the tabs. When you tick the last step you could do in a project, the Undo bar asks once whether it is finished. The tidy pass suggests projects at the top, with Orange Keep and Not a project; under More, Park and Finished. Each press is saved at once and can be undone for ten seconds. At the foot, folded: tasks dated after the five days that are in no project, and tasks dated again and again (Repeating tasks), one row each.
  • PLANOn a day with a plan, the plan's blocks are the list. Today carries solid green Plan my day (and Restructure day once locked in). Tomorrow and the days after carry pale dashed green Prelim plan (or Re-sketch prelim) instead: it copies a prompt to rough in that future day's blocks and move overflow to the next day using your What can move rule, collapsing morning hygiene steps into one coarse window while keeping prelim-marked work slots. When a prelim day becomes Today, it wears a dashed Prelim stamp until Plan my day expands the morning tick-boxes and locks Today in. Each block is a heading (colour, times, label; the one running says Now), and its cards follow in the plan's order, each with its planned time and a small square in its hands colour. A plan step with no task behind it (a morning routine step, Lunch, Plug in devices) is a row with one tick box and nothing else; the tick is kept on the day plan, never as a task. A planned task whose card is not on this tab is one dashed line saying where it went (Moved to Tue 17, Done, With an AI), with no tick box, so a tick always lands on the task itself. Cards due this day that no block holds sit in Not in the plan, straight after the block that is running. Passed blocks fold to one row, saved for every device, but anything still open in them stays in view.
  • HIDEOn Today, the eye at a row's right end hides it from today. It changes nothing about the task. While anything is hidden, the switch at the top of the list says how many rows are hidden and how many of them are still open; press it to show them, hatched, and press an eye again to bring one back. A task that blocks another has no eye. Tomorrow starts with nothing hidden.
  • Orange edge. A task brought in from outside these days because it blocks one here. Its line says which.
  • Faded. Carried over from an earlier day and still open. Not urgent, just not done yet.
  • Navy card, white text. With an AI: the task sits on the account's With Claude list and an AI has it; its line says queued, since when AI work started, or since when an AI chat you opened has it. The tick box ticks it on the With Claude list; the one Orange button is Hand over or Take it back.
  • Pale green card. A review an AI run handed back: it finished a group of your tasks in one go, ticked them, and this one task holds the output for you to review, send or decide. Not the heavy green "ready for you" frame.
  • Hatched. Marked seen with the Seen button: skip it when scanning.
  • Orange button. The press changes the task in Five Days: Hand over, Take it back, Seen, Park, Unpark, Drop, Reject, Skip, Sent myself, −1 day, +1 day.
  • Green button. The press copies the prompt to your clipboard for you to paste into a new AI chat, stamps "In an AI chat since <day date time>" at the top of the note, and moves the task to the With Claude list: Send, Apply, Edit, and Do it / Ask me first / Quick close under Close out. Improve prompts, Plan my day, Restructure day and the pale dashed Prelim plan button copy prompts without moving cards.
  • Copy redo prompt. Under a drafted WhatsApp or email reply, type what should change and copy a prompt for an AI chat to rewrite the draft where it lives (the task, or the Gmail draft). Nothing is sent.
  • White button. The press only shows more or changes nothing: More, Copy, To bottom. The small picture on every button is the verb; two buttons of one colour never share a picture.
  • Red edge. The task has no title. Give it one in Five Days or delete it.
  • Yellow striped strip on a later day. Only today is read live when the page opens. Tomorrow and the days after show your tasks as they were at your last read, with its day and time, so they may be out of date; their tabs have a dashed edge. Press Fetch on the strip to read just that day. It turns green with the time it was read. Each device keeps its own last read.

The line under a task

  • meNo picture. It waits on you: what you need to do or decide.
  • A person. Someone else has the next move, for example a reply you are waiting for. Skip it for now.
  • A lit laptop. A scheduled run, a script or an AI session has the next move. Skip it for now.
  • REDRed words. It names a task or event the page can't find. Check the spelling of the "Blocked by:" line in the task's note.
  • BLOCKSBlocks: … Other tasks wait on this one. Doing it frees them.

Buttons on a card

  • Tick box, left of the name. Marks the task done in Five Days. An Undo bar stays on screen for ten seconds for Tick, Sent myself, Drop/Reject/Skip, Close out / chat handoffs, and Park.
  • Saving, then NOT SAVED. Every press is written to the store at once or not at all. If the store has not answered within a second, the Saving line names the press. If there is still no answer after ten seconds, the press is NOT SAVED: the card goes back to how it was, gets a heavy red outline and says "Not saved" at its top, and the notice strip lists every press not saved, with one Try again. A later press on that card that does save takes it off the list. The notice goes once the list is empty.
  • BARThe button bar runs along the foot of every card. The task's name is plain text, so tapping it does nothing; Copy carries the task's details if you need them.
  • +1 day+1 day. Pushes the due date in Five Days to the day after the one the card is on (an overdue card goes to tomorrow). The button then shows the new day; tap again to push one more.
  • −1 day−1 day. On the four later tabs only: pulls the due date one day earlier, never before today.
  • CopyCopy. Copies the task's full title, due date, note and where it lives, ready to paste.
  • Close outClose out. The main prompt button on every card; the rest sit behind More. Opens three options: Do it, Ask me first, Quick close, plus Cancel (Do it is pre-selected when Who names Claude, Ask me first when only you). Do it, Ask me first and Quick close copy the prompt to your clipboard, write "In an AI chat since <day date time>" at the top of the note and move the task to the With Claude list, so the card leaves your cards for the With an AI band; Send, Apply and Edit do the same. On a WhatsApp reply card the main button is the white Open in WhatsApp: it opens the chat on the device in your hand with the draft typed in, and you press Send there; then Sent myself ticks the card.
  • Improve promptsImprove prompts. Sits next to the main button on every card that has Close out. Use it only on a task whose usual prompts are too general. One press copies a prompt that starts with /prompt-bench and already answers every question the skill asks; the card does not move and nothing is saved. Paste it into a Claude chat with the Five Days connector: it rewrites this task's Do it and Ask me first prompts with your prompt-bench skill, keeping the hard stops and the result line and cutting every other instruction this task does not need. It shows you both, with a reason for each cut, and saves them only on your yes. From then on Close out copies the improved prompts, its Do it and Ask me first buttons wear a white Improved tab with the same page-and-pencil mark, its menu says "improved by you", and the hourly prompt writer leaves them alone.
  • Hand overHand over. On a queued With Claude card: writes "AI working since <day date time>" at the top of the note and marks the card's line "with AI since <time>".
  • Take it backTake it back. On a With Claude card an AI chat or run has: moves the task back to My Tasks, removes the "working since" or "In an AI chat since" line and sets "Who: Me", so it stays with you; the card returns to your cards at once.
  • ParkPark. Writes "Parked by me <date>" first in the note and moves the task's due date to 2099-12-31 so every run skips it; it waits in the Parked band under Today's carry-overs until you tap Unpark (dated today again). Press Undo within ten seconds to put it straight back.
  • DropDrop. Under More on your own cards. For a task you have decided not to do: writes "Dropped by me <date>" first in the note and moves the task to the Dropped list of its account in the Five Days store. It is never ticked, so a tick always means done. Reject on an Upwork card and Skip on a job card do the same with their own line. Press Undo on the bar that appears for ten seconds to put it straight back. On a task moved with +1 day three times or more, Park and Drop come out of More and sit on the card's bar, beside Close out.
  • SeenSeen. Marks the card as seen, so it turns hatched and you can skip it; tap again to unmark.
  • ↓ To bottomTo bottom. Moves the card to the foot of the day's list, under the foldable "Moved to the bottom" band. The task in Five Days is not changed. The page remembers it on every device until you tap ↑ Back up.
  • 1–6 · R · CKeyboard shortcuts. Keys 1 to 5 switch between the five day tabs (Today through day five) and 6 opens Projects; r refreshes the live read and c puts the cursor in the catch box, when no text field or sheet is open.

Claude's tasks: the spark

  • Dashed spark. Marked for AI work. The task's note says "Who: Claude" (an AI does all of it and ticks it) or "Who: Claude, then me" (an AI does the prep, you finish).
  • Yellow: AI working. A run or AI chat has it right now.
  • Green: ready for you. The card gets a heavy green frame, lands in Say yes, and today's header counts these in a green "ready for you" stamp. The prep is done. Tap the badge to open what the AI made; the line beside it says what is left for you.
  • Red: AI needs you. The card gets a heavy red frame. The line beside it says what is missing. Answer it in the task's note or a chat.
  • MINETo keep a task yours, change its Who line to "Who: Me".
  • White dot in a pulse square. An AI finished and ticked that task.

Today's pulse

  • 29Done by me. Tasks you finished today, here or in a chat; a Navy stamp beside it counts the ones an AI finished and ticked. A task you drop, reject or skip goes to the Dropped list and never counts as done.
  • 28Pts. The value of today's finished tasks, from each task's Pts line. A task with no Pts line gets an early score from the prompt writer in the store.
  • LANESThree lanes, named on the left. Me: one square per task you ticked today, at the time you ticked it, coloured by its topic. Claude: tasks an AI finished and ticked (white with a dot). At this pace: your open tasks, each where it would be done at this pace, laid end to end from now (or from 04:00 on later days) around your timed events. Later day tabs show only At this pace. Squares closer than 44px in a lane group into a numbered cluster square so every tap target stays full size.
  • Dashed squares in At this pace. Full colour, by the task's hands band: Say yes, Send a message, Write a thing, Pick one, Pay or move money, Sit at a screen, Get up and do it, and Unsorted (no fill). Stored estimates in mins/ set a task's length; any task not estimated yet counts at a 20-minute default. Blocked tasks go last.
  • Striped stretches in At this pace. Your timed events, with the event's name written inside when wide enough (or on hover/tap when narrow). No task time is laid there.
  • +3Red box right of the strip. "+3 won't fit today": open tasks that would run past 20:00. They are not drawn on the strip. Tap the box for a plain list of them with their minutes; tap it again to close.
  • Tap any square in any lane for a small card: the task's name (or all tasks in a numbered cluster), the time it was done or would land, its minutes and its points if it has any. Hovering shows the same. One card is open at a time; tap anywhere else to close it.
  • Orange line. Now. The strip runs from 04:00 to 20:00.
  • 15:40Lands by. When the last open task would be done; on a day where everything fits, the Navy dashed line marks it on the strip. If tasks would run past 20:00 the stamp turns red and counts them, and the red box beside the strip lists them. It is a forecast, not a target: tasks you leave open on purpose carry over as before.
  • Progress ring. Finished against everything on the day (green yours, Navy AI's), with the percentage and the count in the middle.

Bands: where you planned to be

A band is a Google Calendar event titled "Band <colour>: <place>", for example Band yellow: Gym, with one task per line in its note. It colours the left rail and the canvas for that stretch; tap it for its tasks in order. On a day with a plan, the rail shows the plan's blocks instead, and tapping one jumps to it in the list. A dashed edge means the day planner suggested it; it turns solid once "Suggested by the day planner" is gone from the note.

  • Green: Home.
  • Green hatched: Garden.
  • Yellow: Gym.
  • Grey-blue: Office.
  • Lines hatched: Travel and away.
  • Orange: Friends.
  • Navy: Home night.
  • Ink: Not available.
  • The timeline's background shows where you planned to be.

Your repeating day

Your morning routine, plan lists, questions, rules (including What can move for prelim overflow) and notes live here, in the shared store, one record each. Full day plans put each routine item in as its own tick-box; prelim plans fold personal routine items into one morning window and keep only items set to Own slot in prelim plans. Plants and Morning patio check wait for first light, worked out for Cape Town each day.

When the Machine Runs

Everything that reads or writes your tasks in the Five Days store without you doing it. The page itself never calls an AI. AI work is done by the scheduled Claude runs, which leave their answers in the store,. The rows marked No AI are plain jobs on the server. The State column is read from the store each time you open this section: On time means the run saved a start time in its last slot, Late means it missed that slot, and Off by choice means you switched it off. To switch a run off or back on, tell any chat; it changes one record, settings/runsOff.
